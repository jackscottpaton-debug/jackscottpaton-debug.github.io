About

Jack Paton is a Glasgow-based artist, performer, musician and magician. His work moves between live art, experimental music, clown, theatre, poetry and sleight of hand.

He is a founding member of L, whose performances use interruption, repetition, apology, unstable structures and abrupt changes of frame to make the conditions of performance visible. His solo practice asks how a performance can acknowledge its own construction without losing its liveness.

Jack trained in fine art at Duncan of Jordanstone College of Art & Design and has since worked across galleries, music venues, radio, printed matter and magic. He lives and works in Glasgow.
